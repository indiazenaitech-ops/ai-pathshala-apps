Handwriting practice worksheets for kids: free alphabet tracing sheets

Most tracing sheets on the internet are only in English, or they ask for a login. Here you can make your own sheet in a minute: English letters, numbers, the Hindi varnamala or another Indian alphabet, or your child's name, in a size that suits small hands.

Short answer

Open Handwriting Practice Sheets. Under “What to practise” choose English letters, numbers, an Indian alphabet or “My own words or a name”. Tap the letters to keep only today's letters, set “Letter size” and press “Print / Save as PDF” to print on A4 or save a PDF.

A tracing sheet in 5 steps

  1. Choose what to practise

    Open Handwriting Practice Sheets. Under “What to practise” choose “English: capital letters”, “English: small letters” or “Numbers 0 to 9”, or the letters of Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam or Urdu. The preview changes at once.

  2. Pick the letters

    Tap a letter to take it off the sheet or to put it back. “All” and “None” are quick, and for most Indian alphabets you can also pick “Vowels” or “Consonants”. Four or five letters are enough for one day.

  3. Or type the child's name

    Choose “My own words or a name” and type one word or name on each line. Children like to trace their own name first.

  4. Set the size and style

    Under “Letter size”, start small children on “Very large”. Choose “Dotted” or “Light grey” letters to trace, and whether a row has letters till the end or empty space to write alone. “Show a dot where the letter starts” helps with English letters and numbers. “Add Name and Date lines” gives space for the child's name and the date.

  5. Print or save as PDF

    Press “Print / Save as PDF” and choose A4 paper. Choose Save as PDF as the printer to keep the sheet, or to send it on WhatsApp and print it at a shop.

Tips for handwriting practice

  • Short and regular is best: 10 to 15 minutes every day works better than one long sitting.
  • The first letter in each row is the model. The child writes over the faint letters, then writes alone in the empty space.
  • Start with very large letters. Use smaller letters and more rows as the child gets better. English sheets have four lines; Indian scripts get guide lines that suit the script.
  • Before pencil and paper, let small children trace big letters with a finger, in sand or on a slate. Watch how the child holds the pencil, and praise the effort.

Frequently asked questions

Are these handwriting worksheets free?

Yes. Make and print as many sheets as you like. There is no sign-up and no watermark.

Can I make Hindi varnamala tracing sheets?

Yes. Choose Hindi under “What to practise” and pick the vowels (अ आ इ) or the consonants (क ख ग). It works the same way for Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Urdu.

Can I make a name tracing sheet?

Yes. Choose “My own words or a name” and type the child's name, or a few words, one on each line.

Which age are these sheets for?

For children who are starting to write, roughly from nursery to Class 2, and for older children who want neater writing. Choose the letter size that suits the child.

Why do the letters look a little different on another phone?

The sheet uses the fonts that are already on your device, so the shape of the letters can change a little from one device to another. Look at the preview before you print.
