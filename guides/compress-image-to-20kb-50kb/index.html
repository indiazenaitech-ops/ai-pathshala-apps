How to compress an image to 20 KB or 50 KB

The form says the image must be under 50 KB, and your phone photo is 3 MB. Here is how to bring it under the limit in a few taps, without uploading the photo anywhere.

Updated 14 November 2026 · 4-minute read

🖼️ Open Image Compressor & Resizer (free) See the steps ↓

Short answer

Open Image Compressor & Resizer, press “Choose photos” and choose the size “Same size, smaller file”. Under compression, choose “By file size” and type 20, 50 or the limit you need in “Maximum file size (KB)”. The app picks the best quality that fits. Download the photo and check its size.

Compress a photo in 5 steps

  1. Add your photos

    Open Image Compressor & Resizer and press “Choose photos”. You can add up to 100 photos at a time: JPG, PNG, WebP or iPhone HEIC.

  2. Choose the size

    To keep the same width and height, choose “Same size, smaller file”. If the form asks for exact pixels, choose “Custom size” and type the width and height.

  3. Keep JPG and compress by file size

    Keep the file type on JPG: it is accepted almost everywhere. Under compression, choose “By file size”.

  4. Type the KB limit

    In “Maximum file size (KB)”, type 20, 50, 100 or whatever your form allows. The app tries different quality levels and keeps the best one that fits. If that is not enough, it also makes the photo a little smaller.

  5. Check and download

    Every photo shows its size before and after. Tap a photo to see “Before / after”. Then press “Download one by one” or “Download all (ZIP)”.

Tips for very small files

  • A 20 KB file cannot hold much detail. Crop to the important part or choose a smaller pixel size first: the picture will look sharper at the same KB.
  • A PNG cannot be squeezed to a KB limit. Choose JPG, or WebP if the website accepts it.
  • For exam and job forms that ask for exact pixels, a KB limit and DPI, Form Photo & Signature Resizer is easier: the form sizes are built in.
  • Need a small PDF instead of a picture? Use PDF Compress & JPG to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reduce a photo to 20 KB?

Choose “By file size” and type 20 in “Maximum file size (KB)”. If the photo is large, the app also reduces its pixel size so that it fits. For a clearer result, crop the photo or pick a smaller size first.

And to 50 KB or 100 KB?

The same way: type 50 or 100. You can type any limit from 5 KB upwards.

Will the photo look bad?

A smaller file means less detail. The app always chooses the best quality that fits under your limit, and “Before / after” lets you check before you download.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. They are processed inside your browser and never leave your device. Hidden details such as the GPS location are also removed from the new file.

Can I compress many photos together?

Yes, up to 100 at a time. Download them one by one, or all together as a ZIP file.
