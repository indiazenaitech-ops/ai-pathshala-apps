Hindi voice typing: how to turn speech into text for free

Tired of typing long messages, notes or letters? Record your voice, or pick a WhatsApp voice note, and let the AI write it down. Everything runs on your own device, so private audio stays private.

Updated 14 November 2026 · 5-minute read

Short answer

Open Voice Note to Text, press “Record with mic” and speak (or press “Choose a file” for a voice note). Choose the language you spoke, pick the “Small” model for Hindi in Hindi letters, and press “Convert to text”. Read the text, correct the wrong words, then copy it or download “Text (.txt)”. English comes out very accurate; Hindi is decent with “Small” and needs a quick check.

Speech to text in 5 steps

  1. Record your voice or add a file

    Open Voice Note to Text. Press “Record with mic”, speak clearly and press “Stop and use”. Or press “Choose a file” and pick a WhatsApp voice note, a recording or a video (up to 30 minutes).

  2. Choose the spoken language

    Under “Language spoken in the audio”, choose Hindi, English or another language. Automatic detection also works, but choosing it yourself is more reliable.

  3. Pick the AI model

    “Tiny” and “Base” are fast and good for clear English. For Hindi in Hindi letters, choose “Small”: it is the most accurate, but it is a big download and slow, so a laptop or computer is better than a phone.

  4. Convert to text

    Press “Convert to text” and keep the page open. The first time, the AI model is downloaded once (Wi-Fi is better). The text appears part by part.

  5. Correct it and use it

    Tap a time to listen to that part again, and tap the text to fix a word. Then copy the text, or download “Text (.txt)”, or “Subtitles (.srt)” for a video.

How accurate is it? An honest note

  • English: very good with every model, even on a phone.
  • Hindi: decent with “Small”. Expect some wrong words, especially names, numbers and mixed Hindi-English, so read the text once before you send it.
  • On a phone, “Small” can be too heavy and may stop with an error. “Base” works there, but writes Hindi with rough spelling.
  • This is not live typing: you speak first and the text comes afterwards. Speak clearly, one person at a time, with little background noise. Odia is not available yet.

Frequently asked questions

Can it write in Hindi letters when I speak Hindi?

Yes, with the “Small” model. The smaller models are less reliable for Hindi: “Base” gives rough spelling. For English, every model writes well.

Is my voice uploaded anywhere?

No. Only the AI model is downloaded to your device, once. Your recordings and voice notes are never uploaded, so it is safe for private messages and meetings.

How do I turn a WhatsApp voice note into text?

Long-press the voice note in WhatsApp, tap Share and save it to your phone. Then pick it here with “Choose a file” and press “Convert to text”.

Which other languages work?

With “Small”, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil and Urdu are written in their own letters. Other Indian languages come out in English letters; for them “Translate to English” is often more useful. Odia is not available yet.

How long does it take?

It depends on your device and the model. On a phone, one minute of audio can take a few minutes. The first run also downloads the model once: from about 70 MB (“Tiny”) to about 280 MB (“Small”).
